§ 01

Summary

Warden (“the App”) is an Atlassian Forge app that participates in Atlassian's Runs on Atlassian program. It runs entirely inside your Atlassian cloud environment. It does not send your data to Slate's servers or to any third party — there are no external servers, no analytics calls, and no data egress. The App is read-only: it audits and reports on your page restrictions; it never changes them.

§ 02

Data we access

The App reads only the data required to audit page restrictions, using the granted Atlassian scopes — all read-only, in-tenant, on your behalf:

read:space:confluence Enumerate spaces.
read:page:confluence Walk the page tree (page id, title, parent). The App does not read page bodies or content.
read:content-details:confluence Read which pages are view/edit-restricted and to which users or groups, plus whether those users are still active. This is the READ scope for restrictions; read:content.restriction is Atlassian's write scope and the App does not request it.
read:group:confluence Group membership, to detect empty groups and orphaned group references.
read:user:confluence Resolve the accounts named in restrictions and detect deactivated or orphaned users.
storage:app Store the scan snapshot.

Warden is an administrative audit tool: it reads restriction metadata across the site to produce the report, and does not read the content of your pages.

§ 03

Data we store

The App stores a scan snapshot and its configuration in Atlassian-hosted Forge storage, which lives inside your Atlassian cloud environment and inherits its data-residency region. The snapshot contains only what the report shows: the list of restricted pages (page id and title, not page content), the users and groups named in those restrictions (account and group identifiers and names), the direct-versus-inherited relationships, and the red-flag findings.

Only restricted pages are stored, not your whole page corpus. We do not copy this data outside Atlassian.

§ 04

Data we do not collect

We do not sell, rent, or share your data. We do not use it for advertising or to train machine-learning models. We do not receive a copy of it — it never leaves your Atlassian tenant. We do not read your page content, and we never modify your restrictions.

§ 05

Sub-processors

None beyond Atlassian itself, the platform hosting the App. Because the App runs on Atlassian with no egress, no additional sub-processors are involved.

§ 06

Security

The App inherits Atlassian's platform security controls, including SOC 2 Type II and ISO/IEC 27001, and Atlassian's data-residency options. The audit report is an administrative surface, available to your Confluence site administrators.

Read-only is a fact, not a promise. There is no write scope in Warden's Forge manifest, so the App cannot modify a page restriction even if it were asked to — and any administrator can verify that on the install screen before approving it. The manifest also declares no external permissions and no web triggers, so the restriction data it reads has nowhere to go outside your tenant.

§ 07

Data retention and deletion

The scan snapshot and configuration are stored only while the App is installed. Uninstalling the App removes the App's stored data from your Atlassian environment.

Because the snapshot caches display names for the accounts named in restrictions, the App reports those accounts to Atlassian every week using Atlassian's personal data reporting API. The report goes to Atlassian, in-tenant — nothing is sent to Slate or to any third party. When Atlassian reports that an account has been closed (erased) or updated, the App immediately discards its cached copy of that person's display name; the row then shows the account identifier alone until the next scan resolves a current name. This is how a right-to-erasure request reaches the App's stored data without you having to ask us.
