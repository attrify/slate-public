§ 01

Summary

Pact (“the App”) is an Atlassian Forge app that participates in Atlassian's Runs on Atlassian program. It runs entirely inside your Atlassian cloud environment. It does not send your data to Slate's servers or to any third party — there are no external servers, no analytics calls, and no data egress.

§ 02

Data we access

The App reads only the data required for its features, using the granted Atlassian scopes:

read:jira-work Read your Jira issues and their change history — to compute working-time SLA state (elapsed, remaining, at risk, breached) per policy.
send:notification:jira Email the reporter and assignee on at-risk and breach events, using Jira's own in-tenant notification queue. These emails are sent by Jira, not by an external mail service.
storage:app Store your SLA policies and settings.

It accesses this data on your behalf, within your Atlassian tenant, and only to compute and deliver the features you use.

§ 03

Data we store

To evaluate SLAs and avoid duplicate alerts, the App stores app configuration — the SLA policies you define (statuses, working-hour budgets, calendars, notify settings) and a small per-issue notification-state marker recording which alert has already been sent — in Atlassian-hosted Forge storage, which lives inside your Atlassian cloud environment and inherits its data-residency region. We do not copy your data outside Atlassian.

§ 04

Data we do not collect

We do not sell, rent, or share your data. We do not use it for advertising or to train machine-learning models. We do not receive a copy of it — it never leaves your Atlassian tenant. The App does not integrate Slack, webhooks, or any external destination.

§ 05

Sub-processors

None beyond Atlassian itself, the platform hosting the App. Because the App runs on Atlassian with no egress, no additional sub-processors are involved. At-risk and breach emails are delivered by Atlassian's own Jira notification system.

§ 06

Security

The App inherits Atlassian's platform security controls, including SOC 2 Type II and ISO/IEC 27001, and Atlassian's data-residency options. SLA state is computed from the issue changelog on demand using the App's granted read access within your tenant.

Alerts without egress. Every SLA email leaves through Jira's own notification queue, inside your tenant. Pact's Forge manifest declares no external permissions and no web triggers, so there is no Slack integration, no webhook, and no code path that could carry an alert — or the issue data in it — to a third party.

§ 07

Data retention and deletion

Policy and notification-state data is stored only while the App is installed. Uninstalling the App removes the App's stored data from your Atlassian environment.

§ 08

Children

The App is a business tool and is not directed to children under 16.

§ 09

Changes
