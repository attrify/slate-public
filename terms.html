§ 04

Security measures

Every Slate app is an Atlassian Forge app in the Runs on Atlassian program. It executes inside your own Atlassian cloud environment and declares no external permissions and no web triggers, so there is no code path by which your data can leave your Atlassian tenant. The apps therefore inherit Atlassian's platform security controls, including SOC 2 Type II and ISO/IEC 27001, and Atlassian's data-residency options.

Each app requests the narrowest scopes its features need, and those scopes are shown to an administrator on the install screen before installation is approved. Warden requests read-only scopes only, and so cannot modify anything it audits.

Slate never receives your data. Because the apps run in-tenant with no egress, Slate operates no servers that hold customer data, and there is nothing for Slate to breach, sell, or hand over. This is a property of how the apps are built, not a promise about how they are operated.

§ 05

Data protection

What each app reads and stores is set out in its privacy policy, linked from its Marketplace listing. In summary: apps read only what their declared scopes allow, store derived data in Atlassian-hosted Forge storage inside your tenant, and copy nothing outside Atlassian. Uninstalling an app removes its stored data from your environment.

Slate engages no sub-processors. Atlassian hosts the apps as part of its own platform; there is no third party in the chain.
